We all have our suspicions when someone sends you a message that feels a bit too AI.

Foist turns that tiny moment of Slack suspicion into a shared joke. It does not shame the sender or claim to know who wrote what. It simply examines the prose, pins some clues to the board, and gives you the option to escalate the bit. All in good fun, of course.
